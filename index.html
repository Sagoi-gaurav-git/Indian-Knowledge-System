What is IKS?

India's Living Intellectual Heritage

Indian Knowledge System (IKS) refers to the vast body of knowledge developed on the Indian subcontinent over millennia — spanning science, mathematics, medicine, philosophy, arts, architecture, and governance.


From the concept of zero to the discovery of gravity, from Ayurveda to Yoga — India's contributions have shaped the entire world. This project aims to bring that knowledge alive for the modern generation.

Historical Journey

Timeline of Indian Knowledge

A civilisation that never stopped thinking, discovering, and creating.

3300 BCE – 1300 BCE

Indus Valley Civilisation

Planned cities (Mohenjo-daro, Harappa), advanced drainage systems, standardised weights & measures, and proto-writing. World's first urban planning.

1500 BCE – 500 BCE

Vedic Period

Composition of the Vedas, Upanishads, and early mathematical texts (Sulbasutras). Panini writes Ashtadhyayi — the world's first formal grammar system.

600 BCE – 200 CE

Classical Period

Rise of Buddhism and Jainism. Charaka writes Charaka Samhita (medicine). Kautilya writes Arthashastra. Sushruta performs rhinoplasty — the world's first surgery.

320 CE – 550 CE

Gupta Golden Age

Aryabhata discovers zero and calculates pi. Varahamihira writes on astronomy. Nalanda University becomes the world's first university, attracting scholars from across Asia.

600 CE – 1200 CE

Medieval Achievements

Brahmagupta's algebra. Bhaskara II's calculus concepts. Classical temple architecture at its peak. Indian numerals spread to Arabia and then Europe as "Arabic numerals."

1200 CE – Present

Resilience & Revival

Despite invasions and colonisation, India's knowledge traditions survived. Today, IKS is being systematically documented and integrated back into modern education and science.

India's Global Impact

Key Contributions to the World

India didn't just develop knowledge — it shared it. These innovations changed human civilisation forever.

0

Invention of Zero

Aryabhata (499 CE) — without zero, modern computing, physics, and mathematics would be impossible.

π

Value of Pi

Aryabhata calculated pi to 4 decimal places — more accurate than any European mathematician of his era.

1st

Surgery in History

Sushruta (600 BCE) performed cataract surgery, plastic surgery, and documented 300+ surgical instruments.

10

Decimal Number System

India gave the world the base-10 number system — the foundation of all modern mathematics and science.

∞

Concept of Infinity

Jain mathematicians (400 BCE) were the first to classify and work with different types of infinity.

UNESCO

Yoga — World Heritage

India's gift of Yoga is practiced by 300 million+ people worldwide and is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.
